**Last Updated**: September 2026  
**Developer**: Irfan Haider Abir (iabir2230474)

## 1. Overview
The **UIU UCAM Evaluation Automator** is a client-side productivity tool designed specifically for students of United International University (UIU) to automate the course evaluation process on the official UCAM portal (`https://ucam.uiu.ac.bd/`).

## 2. Information We Collect
**None.** The extension does **NOT** collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, login credentials, browsing history, or analytics with any third-party servers or external databases.

## 3. Data Storage & Usage
- **Login Credentials**: If you choose to enable the *"Save Login Info"* option, your Student ID and Password are stored **strictly on your local device** using Chrome's built-in secure local storage (`chrome.storage.local`).
- **Data Transmission**: All interactions occur directly between your browser and the official UIU UCAM website (`https://ucam.uiu.ac.bd/`) over secure HTTPS. No external communication takes place.

## 4. Permissions Justification
- **`storage`**: Used exclusively to save user preferences and optionally save login credentials locally on the user's browser.
- **`activeTab`**: Used to interact with the currently active UIU UCAM tab during evaluation.
- **`host_permissions` (`https://ucam.uiu.ac.bd/*`)**: Strictly restricted to the UIU UCAM domain for performing course evaluation automation.

## 5. User Control & Deletion
You can delete any saved data at any time by:
1. Turning off the *"Save Login Info"* switch in the extension popup.
2. Clicking the *"Clear"* button in the Activity Log.
3. Uninstalling or removing the extension from Chrome.
